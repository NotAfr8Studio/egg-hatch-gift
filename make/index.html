Changed your mind about something above? The QR code updates by itself.

Nothing gets uploaded. Everything, even the photo, gets packed into the link itself. Right here on your device. There's no copy anywhere else, so hang on to the link.

Anyone with the QR code or link can open the egg, photo and all, and the gift once it hatches. So keep it between you and the birthday person.

Their progress saves on their own phone. Want to test it first? Go for it, your taps stay on your phone.
