EGG HATCH GIFT

Make a birthday egg for a friend. They tap it until it hatches into a dancing little character with their face, and then they get your gift card.

1WHO IS IT FOR?

The egg says: HAPPY BIRTHDAY! FRIEND!

2THEIR FACE

A photo where they look into the camera. It ends up small and pixely on the character, which is half the fun.

No photo? The egg gets a pixel face instead.

That photo could not be opened. Try a JPG or PNG.

Drag the photo until the face fills the shape. Then go to SQUASH.

Squash it, stretch it, drag it around. Wide, tall, tiny or huge all work.

Skin colour

Fills the body and everything around the face. AUTO takes it from the face.

3THE GIFT

Paste the link from the gift card email, without opening it. Opening it and signing in can claim the gift for yourself (Steam does this). Don't have one? Leave it empty: the card then says the present comes another way.

Becomes a REDEEM button next to COPY CODE.

Pick a date

Shown on the card. Steam email gifts must be accepted within 30 days of sending.

Just the egg and the hatch. Handy when you give the present in person afterwards: the card says it comes another way.

4HOW MANY TAPS?

4,000 worked well at a birthday party. Five mini games come along the way, at 20, 40, 60, 80 and 90%.

5MAKE IT

OPEN THE EGG

Link copied.

Changed something above? The QR code and link update by themselves.

Nothing gets uploaded. The name, the photo and the gift go into the link itself, made right here on this device. The photo is shrunk to a few hundred pixels first.

Whoever has the QR code or the link can open the egg, and the gift once it hatches. So only give it to the birthday person.

Their progress saves on their own phone. Opening the link yourself to test it is fine: your taps stay on your phone.
